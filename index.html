⚙️ Grown-ups

How this works: letters are taught in the order s a t i p n … (the Jolly Phonics sequence used in schools worldwide), because those six letters already make real words like sat and pin. Letter sounds come before letter names. Every word can be sounded out with letters already learned — no guessing.

Sound: this app speaks with your device's voice (rate slowed for learners). For the pure letter sounds (ssss, not "ess"), please model them yourself — exaggerated and clipped, no added vowel ("mmm" not "muh").

Pace: one level ≈ 5 minutes. Short daily sessions beat long ones. 🌟
