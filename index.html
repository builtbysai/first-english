Grown-ups

Sounds first: letters come in the s a t i p n … order schools use worldwide: those six letters already make real words like sat and pin. We teach letter sounds before letter names, and every word sounds out from letters already learned. No guessing.

The voice: your device's voice, slowed down for learners. The pure letter sounds come from you: stretch and clip them, no added vowel ("ssss" not "ess", "mmm" not "muh").

Pace: about 5 minutes per level. Short daily sessions beat long ones.
